Info

How many people donate?

In the US, about 3% of people donate each year. That is about 6,800,000 people and over 13,000,000 units each year.

Who can Donate blood?

Anybody healthy who is above 17 and weighs at least 110 pounds can donate blood at any blood drives.

Who does this help?

Anybody going through cancer treatment, chronic illnesses, or just surgery can use blood donations.

Where do you donate?

You can often donate at blood drives or hospitals in your area.
